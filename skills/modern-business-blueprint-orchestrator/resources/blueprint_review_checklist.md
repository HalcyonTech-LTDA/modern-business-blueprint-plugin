# Company Conception Blueprint Review Checklist

This checklist is the evaluation gate that a Tier-1 Venture Partner and the **Lead Integrator** execute before giving formal investment approval to any generated business blueprint.

---

## 1. Compliance with Strict Rules & Lead Integrator Mandates

### Lead Integrator Output Rules Audit
- [ ] **Lead Integrator Rule 1 (Synthesis)**: Are all disjointed agent conversational logs, transcripts, and handoff dialogues completely eliminated? Is the final output a single, cohesive, publication-grade Markdown document?
- [ ] **Lead Integrator Rule 2 (Formatting)**: Does the document strictly adhere to the numbered Roman numeral hierarchy (`I` through `VIII`) following the chronological and strategic flow of a formal business manifesto?
- [ ] **Lead Integrator Rule 3 (Tone Consistency)**: Is strict executive candor maintained uniformly across every single section, avoiding any startup clichés, marketing hype, or optimistic fluff?

### Rule 1: Tone, Candor & Enterprise Focus Audit
- [ ] Are all buzzwords and startup jargon purged? (Check for "game-changer", "revolutionary", "all-in-one", "democratizing", "disruptive", "best-in-class").
- [ ] Is the document written in cold, executive business language?
- [ ] Does the document highlight raw operational friction, counterparty risk, and margin challenges honestly?
- [ ] **Product & GTM Rule 1 (Focus)**: Are all B2C tactics, mass marketing, social media ads, and generic marketing completely eliminated?
- [ ] **Product & GTM Rule 1 (Focus)**: Are exact enterprise C-level decision-makers identified with clear buying mandates (CRO, CTO, Head of Ops, CISO/CCO)?
- [ ] **Finance & Venture Rule 1 (Pragmatism)**: Are all vanity metrics (GMV without take rate, pageviews, downloads) strictly banned?
- [ ] **Finance & Venture Rule 1 (Pragmatism)**: Is all headcount hiring and expansion authorized strictly by verified unit economics ($LTV/CAC \ge 3.0\times$, Payback $\le 12\text{ months}$, Margins $\ge 80\%$), never by narrative?

### Rule 2: Markdown Structure Audit
- [ ] Does the document start immediately with `## I. A TESE CENTRAL` defining the contemporary problem?
- [ ] Does it follow with `## II. A EMPRESA` defining the solution and the exact value chain flow (e.g., $\text{DATA} \rightarrow \text{SIGNAL} \rightarrow \text{CONTEXT} \rightarrow \text{DECISION}$)?
- [ ] Does it include Category Design & Anti-Positioning (What it IS vs What it IS NOT)?
- [ ] Does it include Multi-Layered Monetization & Unit Economics?
- [ ] Does it include Moat & Defensibility Strategy?
- [ ] Does it include Section VI (Technical Architecture, Cybersecurity & Corporate Governance) covering Technology & Engineering, Cybersecurity Posture, Legal Framework & Contracts, and Lean Governance?
- [ ] **Product & GTM Rule 2 (Structure)**: Does it include Section VII (Product Roadmap & Go-to-Market Execution Plan) covering:
  - 1. Beachhead Market & Pragmatic Entry Criteria
  - 2. Trojan Horse MVP Wedge (The Entry Product)
  - 3. Land-and-Expand Product Roadmap (Horizontal & Vertical)
  - 4. Enterprise GTM Engine & Commercial Channels
  - 5. Multi-Stage Enterprise Sales Funnel & Replicable CS
- [ ] **Finance & Venture Rule 2 (Structure)**: Does it include Section VIII (Capital Allocation & Execution Milestones) covering:
  - 1. Financial Model & P&L Structure (strictly separating software revenue from data consumption costs)
  - 2. Core KPIs Scorecard (MRR, ARR, NRR, Gross Margin, CAC, Payback, Cloud/Data Cost per Revenue)
  - 3. Funding Strategy (The 4 Gates: Build, Validation, Commercialization, Scale with 12-18 months runway per stage)
  - 4. The Investment Thesis (recurring revenue, land-and-expand, high switching costs, scale economies, derived data)
  - 5. Pragmatic 18-Month Execution Roadmap (Rule 3 Action Plan)

### Rule 3: Economic Constraint & Scalability Audit
- [ ] Does every single proposed technology component tie directly to either:
  1. A billable revenue tier, OR
  2. A quantified COGS/risk reduction?
- [ ] Has any "technology for technology's sake" been identified and deleted?
- [ ] **Product & GTM Rule 3 (Constraint)**: Is there a strict prohibition on custom client software and one-off forks?
- [ ] **Product & GTM Rule 3 (Constraint)**: Is the architecture cleanly partitioned into an immutable horizontal core engine and declarative configuration schemas (YAML/JSON)?
- [ ] **Corporate Architect Rule 3 (Constraint)**: Does the operating company retain 100% ownership of IP, code, domains, and rules engines with clean chain of title?
- [ ] **Corporate Architect Rule 3 (Constraint)**: Are all technological choices documented and structurally independent of vendor lock-in (OCI containers, open storage standards)?
- [ ] **Finance & Venture Rule 3 (Action Plan)**: Does the document conclude with an actionable 18-month roadmap spanning Month 0-2 (Foundation), M2-5 (Build), M5-8 (Validation), M8-12 (Commercialization), and M12-18 (Scale Decision)?

### Corporate Architect Rule 1: Governance & Rule 2: Structure Audit
- [ ] **Rule 1 (Governance)**: Are premature ornamental governance structures (formal boards of directors, external voting seats) strictly prohibited in early stages?
- [ ] **Rule 1 (Governance)**: Is early-stage governance structured around a 60-minute monthly executive decision meeting with 1-page EDRs?
- [ ] **Rule 2 (Structure)**: Does the blueprint include Technology & Engineering, Cybersecurity, Legal Framework, and Corporate Governance?

## 2. Institutional Investment Gates

| Evaluation Gate | Pass Requirement | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category Differentiation** | Must coin a distinct, ownable category moniker; rejects generic labels. | [ ] PASS / [ ] FAIL |
| **Anti-Positioning Rigor** | Explicitly disqualifies at least 5 adjacent low-margin features/services. | [ ] PASS / [ ] FAIL |

| **18-Month Execution Roadmap** | Concrete 5-phase timeline (Foundation $\rightarrow$ Build $\rightarrow$ Validation $\rightarrow$ Commercialization $\rightarrow$ Scale Decision). | [ ] PASS / [ ] FAIL |

**Investment Verdict**:
- **APPROVED FOR CAPITAL ALLOCATION**: All Rules and all 19 Investment Gates pass.
- **REJECTED / CONDITIONAL REVISION**: Any failed gate triggers automatic return to the appropriate specialized agent for refinement.
