# Modern Business Blueprint Orchestrator

This skill coordinates the multi-agent workflow that ingests raw, ambiguous business concepts and synthesizes them into institutional-grade, venture-backed company conception blueprints.

---

## The Multi-Agent Blueprint Workflow

The process follows a deterministic 5-stage orchestration pipeline:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. INTAKE & DECONSTRUCTION (Raw Idea Ingestion)        │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
                            ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 2. STRATEGIC FOUNDATION (Business Strategist Agent)     │
│    - I. A TESE CENTRAL                                 │
│    - II. A EMPRESA (Value Chain: Input -> Decision)     │
│    - Category Design & Anti-Positioning (IS vs NOT)    │
│    - Multi-Tiered Monetization & Unit Economics        │
│    - Non-Feature Moat & Defensibility Architecture     │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
                            ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 3. TECHNICAL & ECONOMIC VALIDATION (Systems Agent)     │
│    - Constraint Rule 3: Zero Tech for Tech's Sake      │
│    - Binding Every Architectural Asset to Monetization │
│    - Marginal COGS Modeling & Margin Expansion         │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
                            ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 4. GO-TO-MARKET & EXPANSION (GTM Engine Agent)         │
│    - Target Buyer & C-Suite Budget Authority           │
│    - CAC Payback & Inbound/Outbound Velocity           │

│    - VC Partner Investment Verdict                     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Agent Responsibilities & Handoff Contracts

### 1. Ingestion Stage
- **Input**: Raw text prompt, unstructured notes, founder pitch, or napkin idea.
- **Action**: Extract target industry, hypothesized buyer, core friction point, and proposed technology.

### 2. Strategic Foundation Stage (Business Strategist Agent)
- **Role**: Tier-1 Venture Capital Partner & Serial Entrepreneur.
- **Skills Activated**:
  - `modern-category-design-and-positioning`
  - `modern-monetization-and-economic-architecture`
  - `modern-moat-and-defensibility-strategy`
- **Output Contract**:
  - `# I. A TESE CENTRAL` (Contemporary problem and macro inflection).
  - `# II. A EMPRESA` (Solution and exact value chain flow: $\text{DATA} \rightarrow \text{SIGNAL} \rightarrow \text{CONTEXT} \rightarrow \text{DECISION}$).
  - Complete Category Definition, Anti-Positioning table, 4-tier revenue model, unit economics table, and defensibility matrix.

### 3. Technical & Systems Validation Stage
- **Role**: Technical Systems Architect.
- **Enforcement**: Strictly enforces **Rule 3 (Constraint)**. Every proposed technological component (graph engine, microservices, vector storage, custom models) must have an explicit business case:
  - Does it generate billable revenue?
  - Does it reduce COGS / latency?
  - Does it create switching costs?
- If the answer is "no", the technology is deleted from the blueprint.

### 4. Synthesis & Review Stage
- **Role**: Managing Partner Review Committee.
- **Enforcement**: Strictly enforces **Rule 1 (Tone)**. Replaces startup buzzwords and marketing fluff with raw economic realities. Verifies all unit economic hurdle rates ($LTV/CAC \ge 3.0\times$, Payback $\le 12\text{ months}$, Margins $\ge 80\%$, $NRR \ge 120\%$).

---

## Execution Guide

When prompted with a raw business idea:
1. Initialize the session and invoke the **Business Strategist Agent**.
2. Execute the Category Design, Monetization, and Defensibility skills sequentially.
3. Validate all technology proposals against monetization rules.
4. Render the unified company conception document starting with `# I. A TESE CENTRAL` and `# II. A EMPRESA`.
