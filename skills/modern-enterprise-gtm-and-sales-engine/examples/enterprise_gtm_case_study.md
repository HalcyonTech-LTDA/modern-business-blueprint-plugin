# Enterprise GTM Case Study: High-Ticket Pipeline Velocity

This case study breaks down how an enterprise decision infrastructure startup built a predictable $4.2M ARR pipeline in 12 months using **Founder-Led Enterprise Sales**, an **Authority Marketing Engine**, and a **Replicable 30-Day Onboarding Playbook**.

---

## 1. Company Baseline & Market Context
- **Product**: Real-time cross-border compliance and fraud decisioning infrastructure.
- **Target Contract Size (ACV)**: $120,000 blended average ($75k base platform + consumption tier).
- **Target Decision-Makers**: Chief Risk Officer (CRO), Chief Compliance Officer (CCO), Chief Technology Officer (CTO).
- **Core Market**: 250 named North American and European cross-border remittance and fintech banking institutions.

---

## 2. Go-to-Market Execution Architecture

### Phase 1: Authority Marketing Operation (Months 1-4)
- **Zero Paid Advertising**: $0 spent on Google search ads, Facebook ads, or generic LinkedIn sponsored posts.
- **Regulatory Deep-Dive Publication**: Authored an authoritative 28-page technical analysis: *"The 2026 FinCEN Modernization Directive: Why Legacy Batch AML Engines Will Fail Supervisory Audits."*
  - Distributed directly to the Named Account List via personalized C-level postal courier packages and targeted outreach from the founder.
  - Generated 42 C-level downloads and 19 direct executive diagnostic requests within 30 days.
- **Executive Salon Series**: Convened two private virtual dinners (10 executives each, Chatham House Rule) discussing real-world examiner friction. 14 of the 20 participating institutions initiated formal vendor evaluations.

### Phase 2: Founder-Led Enterprise Sales Funnel (Months 4-10)
- **Funnel Performance Metrics**:
  - *Named Accounts Targeted*: 120 tier-1 accounts.
  - *Stage 1 (Discovery)*: 38 accounts (31.6% conversion from targeted list).
  - *Stage 2 (Technical & MEDDPICC Qualification)*: 26 accounts (68.4% conversion).
  - *Stage 3 (Scored 14-Day Shadow PoC)*: 19 accounts (73.1% conversion).
    - 18 of the 19 PoCs met the pre-agreed performance charter ($< 30\text{ms}$ latency, $> 70\%$ false positive reduction).
  - *Stage 4 (Legal & InfoSec Review)*: 17 accounts (89.5% conversion).
  - *Stage 5 (Closed-Won Contracts)*: 15 accounts (88.2% conversion).
- **Overall Win Rate (from Stage 1 Discovery to Closed-Won)**: 39.5%.
- **Average Sales Cycle Length**: 84 days (under 3 months from initial discovery to signed contract).

## 4. Key Strategic Insights

1. **Authority Drives C-Level Access**: Cold sales calls to enterprise executives typically yield $< 2\%$ response rates. Founder outreach centered on an authoritative regulatory critique achieved a **34% executive meeting booking rate**.
2. **Pre-Agreed PoC Charters Eliminate Slippage**: Requiring the Economic Buyer to sign the 14-day success criteria charter before provisioning sandbox access prevented deals from stalling in open-ended evaluations.
3. **Replicable Onboarding Accelerates Cash Flow**: Because deployment required only Kafka stream mapping via declarative YAML schemas rather than custom code development, customers achieved live production cutover in 18 days, enabling immediate invoicing.
