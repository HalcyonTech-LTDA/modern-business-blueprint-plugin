# Enterprise GTM & Sales Engine

This skill provides the commercial architecture, sales methodology, and enterprise distribution playbooks for the **Product & GTM Lead Agent** (VP of Product & CRO) to construct a high-velocity, high-ticket B2B enterprise revenue engine.

---

## Core Principles

1. **High-Ticket B2B Enterprise Realities ($50k - $500k+ ACV)**:
   - Selling enterprise infrastructure is not a transactional, self-serve e-commerce motion.
   - It requires orchestrating multi-stakeholder consensus across risk-averse corporate committees.
   - **Rule 1 Enforcement**: Absolute ban on B2C tactics, broad digital advertising, consumer social gimmicks, or low-intent lead generation. Enterprise buyers (CROs, CTOs, Chief Compliance Officers, CISOs) do not click on Facebook ads; they respond to peer proof, regulatory imperative, and undeniable technical authority.

2. **Evolution of the Sales Motion**:
   - **Phase 1: Founder-Led Enterprise Sales (0 to $1M ARR)**:
     - The founders lead every conversation, pitch, technical demo, and procurement negotiation.
     - Direct executive contact creates deep empathy, surfaces real-world objections, and validates product-market fit without intermediate translation loss.
   - **Phase 2: Specialized Enterprise Pods ($1M to $10M ARR)**:
     - Transition to specialized pods: Enterprise Account Executive (AE) paired 1:1 with a Forward Deployed Solutions Architect (FDSA).
     - The AE drives executive consensus and commercial terms; the FDSA conducts architectural reviews, security compliance, and live proof-of-concept (PoC) validation.

3. **Target Account Strategy & Buying Committee Architecture**:
   - Deploy an Account-Based Everything (ABX) motion focused on a tightly curated **Named Account List** (e.g., top 150 accounts in the beachhead segment).
   - Systematically map and address the five distinct roles in every enterprise buying committee:
     1. **The Economic Buyer** (CRO / CFO / CEO): Controls discretionary capital; cares about bottom-line margin expansion, operational leverage, and risk mitigation.
     2. **The Technical Champion** (CTO / VP Engineering / Chief Architect): Evaluates architectural elegance, zero-latency overhead, API design, scalability, and security posture.
     3. **The Operational User** (Head of Operations / Lead Compliance Officer): Touches the daily workflow; cares about false positive reduction, alert fatigue, and usability.
     4. **The Risk & Governance Gatekeeper** (CISO / Chief Compliance Officer / DPO): Protects the firm against data breaches, statutory fines, and regulatory scrutiny.
     5. **Procurement & Legal**: Negotiates MSA/DPA terms, SOC2/ISO audit certifications, and enterprise payment schedules.

4. **Rigorous Multi-Stage Enterprise Sales Funnel**:
   - Implement a 5-stage funnel governed by strict **binary exit criteria** and **MEDDPICC** qualification (Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Paper Process, Identify Pain, Champion, Competition).
   - Stages do not advance based on seller optimism, but on verifiable customer commitments (e.g., signed PoC Charter, shared sandbox data, executive sponsor meeting confirmed).

5. **Authority-Building Marketing: Credibility $\rightarrow$ Education $\rightarrow$ Demand**:
   - Marketing does not generate spam leads; it establishes intellectual and regulatory authority:
     - **Credibility**: Authoring Tier-1 regulatory impact analyses, technical architecture whitepapers, and peer benchmark reports.
     - **Education**: Hosting closed-door, Chatham House Rule executive roundtables for target CROs and CTOs.
     - **Demand**: Inbound inquiries from senior decision-makers seeking guidance on upcoming statutory deadlines or structural performance bottlenecks.

6. **Replicable Customer Success & Onboarding (Ban on Custom Consulting)**:
   - **Rule 3 Enforcement**: Customer Success is an engineered, automated onboarding pipeline—never an open-ended professional services or custom consulting engagement.
   - Enforce a strict **30-Day Time-to-Value (TTV)** standard using pre-built connectors, declarative configuration templates, and automated integration validation scripts.

### Step 1: Build Named Account List & Profile Buying Committee
- Filter the beachhead universe to identify top 100-200 accounts meeting the [Beachhead Qualification Criteria](../modern-beachhead-and-mvp-strategy/SKILL.md).
- Map specific individuals for each account: Name, Title, Reporting Structure, Previous Tech Stack choices.
- Tailor messaging to each stakeholder's core metric (e.g., CRO $\rightarrow$ NRR & gross margins; CTO $\rightarrow$ latency & uptime; Compliance $\rightarrow$ audit fine immunity).

### Step 2: Deploy Authority Engine (Credibility $\rightarrow$ Education $\rightarrow$ Demand)
Execute authoritative content operations:
- Release deep-dive technical benchmarks comparing legacy batch processing against modern real-time streaming architectures.
- Publish quarterly regulatory alert briefs breaking down new statutory enforcement actions and concrete compliance blueprints.
- Host quarterly invitation-only virtual executive roundtables limited to 12 C-level participants.

### Step 3: Execute Founder-Led Outbound & Executive Inbound
Engage target accounts through high-signal, zero-spam channels:
- Highly tailored, research-backed executive outreach referencing the target's public regulatory filings or recent infrastructure shifts.
- Warm investor and advisory board network introductions.
- Inbound inquiries generated by authoritative technical content.

### Step 4: Advance the 5-Stage Funnel with MEDDPICC Qualification
Manage deals through the 5 stages documented in [High-Ticket Sales Funnel & MEDDPICC](./references/high_ticket_sales_funnel_and_meddpicc.md):
- **Stage 1**: Executive Discovery & Pain Quantification.
- **Stage 2**: Architecture Review & Security Pre-Screening.
- **Stage 3**: Scored 14-Day Proof of Concept (PoC) with pre-signed commercial terms.
- **Stage 4**: Legal, Procurement & InfoSec Review.
- **Stage 5**: Closed-Won Contract Execution & Project Kickoff.

### Step 5: 30-Day Replicable Integration & Expansion Handoff
Execute the standardized onboarding methodology documented in [Replicable Customer Success](./references/replicable_customer_success_onboarding.md):
- Day 1-7: Sandbox configuration and test stream verification.
- Day 8-14: Passive Shadow Mode validation against legacy benchmarks.
- Day 15-21: Production cutover and audit trail verification.
- Day 22-30: Formal Value Realization Review with the Economic Buyer.
- Establish baseline usage tracking for land-and-expand triggers.
