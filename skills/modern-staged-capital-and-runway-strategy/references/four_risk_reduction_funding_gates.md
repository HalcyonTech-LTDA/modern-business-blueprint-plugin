# The Four Risk-Reduction Funding Gates

In Tier-1 venture capital, every equity financing stage must correspond to the extinction of a specific category of business risk. This reference outlines the architectural, commercial, and financial criteria governing each of the four funding gates.

---

## Gate 1: Build (Extinguishing Technology & Architectural Risk)

- **The Fundamental Question**: *Can this system physically be engineered to solve the core problem within enterprise latency and security constraints?*
- **Capitalization Profile**:
  - Stage: Pre-Seed / Inception Round.
  - Capital Quantum: $500,000 to $1,500,000.
  - Instrument: Post-money SAFE or convertible note with institutional friendly terms.
  - Target Runway: **12 to 18 Months** (Net monthly burn: $35,000 to $70,000).
- **Core Activities**:
  - Building the horizontal core ingestion engine and rule processing pipeline.
  - Benchmarking stream ingestion SLAs under simulated enterprise workloads ($> 10,000$ transactions/second at $< 25\text{ms}$ latency).
  - Multi-stage environment isolation (Dev, Staging, Prod) and SOC 2 Type I readiness.
  - Securing 100% intellectual property assignment and clean chain of title.
- **Hiring Governance**: Operating founders (CTO, CEO) + 2 core infrastructure engineers. **Zero sales reps or marketing hires.**
- **Gate 1 Binary Exit Hurdle**: A functioning, containerized platform running on staging infrastructure that satisfies the defined latency benchmark without memory leakage or state desynchronization.

---

## Gate 2: Validation (Extinguishing Product-Market & Value Realization Risk)

- **The Fundamental Question**: *Does the software produce quantified, bleeding-neck ROI for a live enterprise customer without breaking their operations?*
- **Capitalization Profile**:
  - Stage: Institutional Seed Round.
  - Capital Quantum: $2,000,000 to $3,500,000.
  - Target Runway: **12 to 18 Months** (Net monthly burn: $110,000 to $180,000).
- **Core Activities**:
  - Deploying the Trojan Horse MVP wedge in **Passive Shadow Mode** inside 2 to 4 design-partner enterprise customers.
  - Proving real-world accuracy: verifying $\ge 70\%$ false-positive reduction and zero latency penalty against incumbent systems.
  - Standardizing the 30-day onboarding runbook and confirming zero custom code forks.
  - Securing SOC 2 Type II audit report to unblock corporate enterprise procurement.
- **Hiring Governance**: Founders + 1 Forward Deployed Solutions Architect (FDSA) to assist customer data mapping. **Banned: Account Executives, SDRs, agency brand marketing.**
- **Gate 2 Binary Exit Hurdle**: Pre-signed commercial conversion agreements or binding Letters of Intent (LOIs) from at least 2 enterprise design partners agreeing to convert to full software licenses upon trial completion.

---

## Gate 3: Commercialization (Extinguishing Repeatable Go-To-Market Risk)

- **The Fundamental Question**: *Can a non-founder sales rep repeatedly sell this software to independent enterprise C-level committees at institutional unit economics?*
- **Capitalization Profile**:
  - Stage: Late Seed / Series A.
  - Capital Quantum: $5,000,000 to $10,000,000.
  - Target Runway: **15 to 18 Months** (Net monthly burn: $280,000 to $450,000).
- **Core Activities**:
  - Scaling from 2 design partners to 10-15 paying enterprise logos across the beachhead segment.
  - Crossing $\$1,000,000$ to $\$2,000,000$ in contracted Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).
  - Verifying unit economics: CAC Payback $\le 12\text{ months}$, Blended Gross Margin $\ge 75\% - 85\%$ (Software Gross Margin $\ge 85\%$), and initial cohort expansion demonstrating NRR $\ge 120\%$.
  - Formalizing the 5-stage MEDDPICC sales funnel and 14-day scored PoC charter.
- **Hiring Governance**: Authorized to hire Pod 1: 1 Senior Enterprise AE + 1 dedicated Solutions Architect + 1 Customer Success Engineer. **Rule 1 Enforcement**: Hiring of Pod 2 is strictly frozen until Pod 1 proves quota attainment and verified $< 12$-month payback.
- **Gate 3 Binary Exit Hurdle**: Demonstrating $\ge \$1.0\text{M} - \$1.2\text{M}$ contracted ARR, verified blended gross margins $\ge 75\% - 85\%$ (software margins $\ge 85\%$), and at least 3 distinct non-founder closed contracts with standard Master Services Agreements.

---

## Gate 4: Scale (Extinguishing Operational Scalability & Market Dominance Risk)

- **The Fundamental Question**: *Can capital be poured into this proven distribution engine to capture horizontal platform market leadership without breaking unit economics?*
- **Capitalization Profile**:
  - Stage: Series B and Beyond.
  - Capital Quantum: $15,000,000 to $30,000,000+.
  - Target Runway: **18 to 24 Months**.
- **Core Activities**:
  - Expanding from Beachhead Module to adjacent modules (Module 2 Continuous Surveillance, Module 3 Automated Reporting, Module 4 Consortium Graph).
  - Cross-vertical expansion (e.g., Remittance $\rightarrow$ Digital Banking $\rightarrow$ Healthcare Claims).
  - Scaling GTM through multiple regional sales pods and enterprise channel alliances.
  - Achieving sustained software gross margins $\ge 85\%$ and Net Revenue Retention $\ge 130\%$.
- **Hiring Governance**: Scaled sales pods, dedicated VP of Sales, product marketing, and customer success management, strictly authorized by quarterly financial audit demonstrating LTV/CAC $\ge 4.0\times$.
- **Gate 4 Binary Exit Hurdle**: Compounding ARR $> \$8\text{M} - \$10\text{M}$ with net burn multiple $< 1.2\times$ and sustained NRR $> 125\%$.
