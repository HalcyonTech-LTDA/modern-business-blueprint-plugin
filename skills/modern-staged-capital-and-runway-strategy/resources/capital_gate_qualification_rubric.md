# Capital Gate Qualification Rubric

Use this binary qualification scorecard to determine whether an enterprise venture has legitimately satisfied its current risk-reduction gate and earned the authorization to raise subsequent capital or scale headcount.

---

## 1. Gate 1 to Gate 2 Qualification (Build $\rightarrow$ Validation)

All four conditions must be marked `[PASSED]` before raising Seed capital or hiring customer-facing staff:

- [ ] **Technical SLA Demonstration**: Working core engine deployed in isolated staging environment achieving target p99 latency SLA under simulated load ($> 5,000$ msg/sec).
- [ ] **Data Source Physical Limit Modeling**: Decoupled ingestion architecture validated against upstream batch/streaming realities with transactional outbox and idempotency keys.
- [ ] **Clean Chain of Title & IP Ownership**: 100% intellectual property assigned to corporate entity in present tense ("hereby assigns") with zero contractor encumbrances.
- [ ] **Runway Discipline Preserved**: Gate 1 completed with at least 3 months of residual cash or pre-arranged seed investor pipeline.

---

## 2. Gate 2 to Gate 3 Qualification (Validation $\rightarrow$ Commercialization)

All five conditions must be marked `[PASSED]` before authorizing the first Account Executive pod or Series A financing:

- [ ] **Design Partner Operational Verification**: At least 2 enterprise design partners deployed in passive shadow mode processing live production traffic.
- [ ] **Quantified Accuracy & ROI**: Demonstrated $\ge 70\%$ false positive / friction reduction compared to incumbent solutions with zero downtime.
- [ ] **Contractual Conversion Commitment**: Signed commercial agreements or binding LOIs with committed ACV $\ge \$50\text{k}$ upon production cutover.
- [ ] **Zero Custom Client Branches**: Implementation achieved strictly via declarative configuration schemas atop the immutable horizontal core engine (**Rule 3 Constraint**).
- [ ] **Cybersecurity Readiness**: SOC 2 Type II audit report delivered or in final review stage to eliminate procurement friction.

---

## 3. Gate 3 to Gate 4 Qualification (Commercialization $\rightarrow$ Scale)

All five conditions must be marked `[PASSED]` before authorizing multi-pod sales scaling or Series B expansion:

- [ ] **Repeatable Sales Velocity**: At least 10 paying enterprise accounts signed by non-founder Account Executives.
- [ ] **Contracted ARR Milestone**: Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue exceeds $\$1,000,000 - \$2,000,000$.
- [ ] **Unit Economic Proof**: Fully loaded CAC Payback period verified at $\le 12\text{ months}$ across the last 2 cohorts.
- [ ] **Retention Expansion**: Net Revenue Retention (NRR) $\ge 120\%$ driven by organic monitored entity growth or module upsells.
- [ ] **Gross Margin Floor**: Consolidated Blended Gross Margin verified at $\ge 75\% - 80\%$ with Software Gross Margin $\ge 85\%$.
