# Economic Engine Case Study: The Multi-Layered Financial Model

This case study outlines the quantitative economic engine of an institutional B2B intelligence platform: **Aether Decision Technologies**.

---

## 1. Customer Cohort Profile: Mid-Market to Enterprise Financial Institutions

### Baseline Unit Economics per Target Customer:
- **Target Buyer**: Chief Risk Officer (CRO) / Chief Compliance Officer (CCO).
- **Sales Cycle**: 75 - 90 days.
- **Contract Duration**: 24 months, billed annually upfront.
- **Initial First-Year Contract Value (ACV)**: **$168,000**.

---

## 2. Multi-Layer Revenue Breakdown for Representative Customer

A mid-sized fintech bank processing 40,000 active corporate accounts and executing 150,000 risk evaluations annually generates the following revenue composition:

| Revenue Layer | Billing Mechanism | Quantity & Rate | Annual Revenue | Gross Margin % |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Layer 1: Base Platform Fee** | Annual Fixed Fee | Single Dedicated Tenant | $72,000 | 90% |
| **Layer 2: Entity Surveillance** | Monthly Recurring | 40,000 entities @ $0.12/mo | $57,600 | 82% |
| **Layer 3: Decision Execution** | Metered Consumption | 150,000 verdicts @ $0.15/ea | $22,500 | 76% |
| **Layer 4: Benchmark Feed** | Annual Module Add-on | Tier-1 Peer Risk Graph | $16,000 | 95% |
| **Total First-Year ACV** | | | **$168,100** | **Blended: 85.3%** |

---

## 3. Fully-Loaded Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Analysis

### Sales & Marketing Cost Allocation (Mid-Market Motion):
- Enterprise Account Executive (OTE $240k, 50% commission): $12,000 per deal closed.
- Sales Engineering Allocation: $4,500 per deal closed.
- Targeted Inbound & Account-Based Marketing (ABM) Ad Spend: $8,000 per customer.
- Legal, Security Audit & POC Cloud Provisioning: $6,000 per customer.
- **Total Fully-Loaded CAC**: **$30,500**.

---

## 4. Key Financial Ratios & Return Metrics

### 1. CAC Payback Period:
$$\text{Gross Margin per Month} = \frac{\$168,100 \times 85.3\%}{12} = \$11,949/\text{month}$$
$$\text{Payback Period} = \frac{\$30,500}{\$11,949} \approx \mathbf{2.55\text{ months}}$$
*(Exceptionally strong capital efficiency, recovering cash outlay in under 3 months due to upfront annual invoicing).*

### 2. Lifetime Value (LTV) Calculation (Assuming conservative 5% annual logo churn over 5-year lifecycle with 125% NRR):
- Year 1 Gross Margin: $\$143,389$
- Year 2 Gross Margin (125% expansion): $\$179,236$
- Year 3 Gross Margin (120% expansion): $\$215,084$
- 3-Year Cumulative LTV: **$537,709**
$$\text{LTV / CAC Ratio} = \frac{\$537,709}{\$30,500} = \mathbf{17.6\times}$$
