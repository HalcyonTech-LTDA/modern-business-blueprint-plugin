# Shareholders' Agreement Mandatory Clauses Framework

The Shareholders' Agreement (SHA) is the foundational legal document governing relations among co-founders and early shareholders. Ambiguity in the SHA invariably results in shareholder litigation, paralyzed cap tables, or uninvestable ventures.

The Corporate Architect Agent must mandate the inclusion of four essential statutory pillars in every early-stage SHA:

---

## 1. Vesting & Reverse Vesting Mechanics

### Standard Schedule:
- **Total Term**: 48 calendar months (4 years).
- **Cliff**: 12 calendar months (1 year).
- **Vesting Distribution**:
  - $0\%$ vests prior to Month 12.
  - $25\%$ vests on the 1st anniversary of the vesting commencement date.
  - The remaining $75\%$ vests in equal monthly increments of $1/48\text{th}$ ($2.083\%$) on the final day of each calendar month across months 13 through 48.

### Acceleration Protocols:
1. **Single-Trigger Acceleration (Prohibited)**: Equity that automatically vests solely upon an acquisition creates severe moral hazard and is universally rejected by institutional acquirers and late-stage investors.
2. **Double-Trigger Acceleration (Mandatory)**: Equity acceleration occurs if and only if:
   - *Trigger 1*: A bona fide Change of Control (sale of $\ge 50\%$ voting stock or substantially all assets) is consummated; AND
   - *Trigger 2*: Within 12 months following the Change of Control, the founder's employment or executive relationship is terminated by the acquiring entity *without cause*, or the founder resigns for *Good Reason* (e.g., material reduction in salary or relocation $> 50\text{ miles}$).
   - *Acceleration Quantum*: $50\%$ to $100\%$ of remaining unvested shares immediately vest.

## 2. Good Leaver vs. Bad Leaver Definitions & Buyback Valuation

The SHA must eliminate subjectivity by defining explicit, binary conditions for departure.

### The Trigger Matrix:

| Category | Trigger Conditions | Unvested Shares Treatment | Vested Shares Treatment & Repurchase Valuation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bad Leaver** | - Conviction of felony or criminal act involving moral turpitude.<br>- Willful fraud, embezzlement, or misappropriation of company funds.<br>- Material uncurable breach of SHA, PIIA, or Non-Compete.<br>- Voluntary resignation prior to completing the 1-year cliff period. | Automatically forfeited and repurchased by the company at nominal price (€0.01 / $0.01 per share or aggregate nominal par value). | The company has the exclusive option to repurchase all vested shares at the **lower of**: (a) nominal par value, or (b) 50% of Book Value (Patrimônio Líquido). |
| **Good Leaver** | - Death or permanent physical/mental incapacity.<br>- Involuntary termination without cause by the company.<br>- Voluntary resignation following completion of the 1-year cliff period with approved transition. | Automatically forfeited and repurchased by the company at nominal price. | Founder retains vested shares, OR company has option to repurchase at **Fair Market Value (FMV)** (determined by recent priced equity round or independent valuation), payable in 12-24 equal monthly installments with statutory interest. |

## 3. Exit Governance: Drag-Along, Tag-Along & Preemption

### Drag-Along Rights:
- **Threshold**: Shareholders holding at least **75% of voting stock** (which must include the approval of the CEO/lead founder).
- **Mechanics**: If the requisite threshold approves a bona fide third-party acquisition offer to purchase the entire company, all other shareholders are legally compelled to vote in favor, sign identical transaction documents, and sell their shares at the identical valuation and terms.
- **Protection**: Eliminates the ability of an estranged ex-employee or disgruntled minor angel investor to hold the transaction hostage.

### Tag-Along Rights:
- **Protection**: If any controlling founder proposes to sell shares to a third party, all other shareholders have the right to join the transaction pro-rata.
- **Pricing Parity**: The third party must purchase the minority shares at 100% identical price per share and terms.

### Right of First Refusal (ROFR) & Preemption:
- Any shareholder desiring to transfer or pledge shares must first provide formal written notice to the company and remaining shareholders.
- The company possesses the first option to repurchase the shares; if declined, the remaining shareholders have pro-rata secondary purchase rights at the proposed transaction price.

---

## 4. Restrictive Covenants: Non-Compete, Non-Solicitation & Exclusivity

1. **Exclusivity of Service**: Operating founders contractually agree to dedicate 100% of their professional time and energy to the company. Prohibits outside consulting, active board roles, or unauthorized side projects.
2. **Post-Departure Non-Compete**:
   - *Duration*: 12 to 24 months following the date of termination.
   - *Scope*: Strictly defined as engaging in, investing in, advising, or operating any business providing continuous decision intelligence or competing infrastructure within the company’s operating markets.
3. **Non-Solicitation of Talent & Clients**:
   - 24-month post-termination covenant prohibiting the direct or indirect solicitation, hiring, or inducement of company employees, contractors, or active enterprise customers.
4. **Liquidated Damages & Injunctive Relief**:
   - Parties acknowledge that a breach of restrictive covenants causes irreparable injury that cannot be remedied solely by monetary damages.
   - The company is entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief without the requirement of posting a bond, plus pre-stipulated liquidated damages (e.g., equivalent to 100% of total salary and equity value received during the prior 12 months).
