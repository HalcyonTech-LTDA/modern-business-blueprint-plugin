# Lean Corporate Governance

This skill provides the corporate governance methodology, executive operating cadence, and foundational shareholder agreement architecture for the **Corporate Architect Agent** (acting as combined Chief Technology Officer and General Counsel) to build an agile, functional corporate foundation while ruthlessly preventing premature governance bureaucracy.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Strict Prohibition of Premature Ornamental Governance (Rule 1 Governance)**:
   - Early-stage companies (pre-seed, seed, post-seed) frequently succumb to "governance theater": establishing formal fiduciary Boards of Directors with external seats, complex voting subcommittees, and ceremonial advisory boards before achieving product-market fit or raising a priced institutional Series A.
   - **The Failure Mode**: Formal boards create massive administrative overhead, legal liability for fiduciary duties, slow decision-making, and give non-operational third parties the ability to block operational pivots.
   - **The Lean Mandate**: Fiduciary control must remain 100% concentrated with the operating founder-executives. Formal Boards of Directors are strictly prohibited until mandated by lead institutional venture capital investors in a priced Series A equity financing.

2. **The Monthly Executive Decision Cadence**:
   - Governance in early-stage infrastructure companies is about **operational velocity and rigorous accountability**, not ceremonial presentations.
   - The primary governance mechanism is the **Monthly Executive Decision Meeting**:
     - Held on the first Tuesday of every month, strictly time-boxed to 60 minutes.
     - Attendees: Operating founders and key executive leaders (CEO, CTO, CRO/COO).
     - Focus: Binary decisions, resource allocation, runway management, and accountability logs.
     - **Ban on Slide Theater**: Presentations via PowerPoint or pitch decks are strictly banned. The meeting operates on pre-read 1-page Executive Decision Records (EDRs) and live operational dashboards.

3. **The Ironclad Shareholders' Agreement (SHA)**:
   - Co-founder alignment cannot rely on informal trust; it must be legally codified in a comprehensive Shareholders' Agreement executed on day one before issuing substantial equity.
   - The SHA must detail four non-negotiable contractual mechanisms:
     - **Vesting & Reverse Vesting**: Standard 4-year vesting schedule with a mandatory 1-year cliff and monthly linear vesting thereafter (1/48th per month). Unvested shares are subject to company repurchase at nominal value upon departure.
     - **Good Leaver vs. Bad Leaver**: Objective, unambiguous triggers defining exit conditions. Bad leavers (fraud, breach of contract, criminal act, voluntary departure before cliff) forfeit unvested shares and have vested shares repurchased at nominal or book value. Good leavers (death, permanent incapacity, termination without cause) retain vested shares or receive fair market value.
     - **Drag-Along & Tag-Along Rights**: High drag-along threshold (e.g., 75% or 80% of voting shares) preventing minority deadlock during an acquisition, coupled with 100% tag-along rights protecting minority shareholders at identical pricing and terms.
     - **Non-Compete, Non-Solicitation & Exclusivity**: 12 to 24-month post-departure non-compete within the company's category, non-solicitation of employees and customers, and mandatory full-time exclusivity during tenure.

4. **Capital Velocity & Cap Table Cleanliness**:
   - Keep the cap table pristine. Avoid issuing equity or advisory grants to non-operational mentors without strict time-based and milestone-based vesting.
   - Consolidate early investor holdings into standardized SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) instruments to avoid premature voting fragmentation.

### Step 1: Governance Stage Audit & Premature Structure Elimination
1. Review corporate charter and statutory documents.
2. Strike any proposed formal external Board of Directors, independent directors, or voting committees for early-stage vehicles.
3. Centralize all statutory management power in the Executive Officers (Administradores / Officers) composed strictly of active founders.

### Step 2: Monthly Executive Decision Meeting Protocol Setup
1. Schedule a recurring 60-minute monthly session.
2. Define the rigid meeting agenda:
   - *00-10m: Financial Health & Runway* (Net burn, cash balance, runway months, AR aging).
   - *10-30m: Strategic Decisions & Trade-offs* (Review pre-submitted 1-page Executive Decision Records).
   - *30-50m: Operational Blockers & Risk Review* (Engineering uptime, deal slippage, compliance bottlenecks).
   - *50-60m: Accountability Log & Commitments* (Record binary owners and deadlines for every agreed action).
3. Enforce the **Zero-Slides Rule**: All discussions center on written narrative memos and live metric dashboards.

### Step 3: Equity Vesting & Reverse Vesting Architecture
1. Subject 100% of founder and employee shares to reverse vesting:
   - Total Vesting Period: 48 months (4 years).
   - Cliff Period: 12 months (25% vests at Month 12).
   - Subsequent Cadence: 1/48th (2.083%) vests monthly on the last day of each month.
2. Configure **Change of Control Acceleration**:
   - Prohibit single-trigger acceleration (where an acquisition automatically vests all equity).
   - Adopt **Double-Trigger Acceleration**: Equity accelerates only if: (a) a change of control occurs AND (b) the founder/executive is terminated without cause or constructively discharged within 12 months following the transaction.

### Step 4: Good Leaver vs Bad Leaver Legal Specification
1. Categorize all possible departures:
   - **Bad Leaver**: Fraud, willful misconduct, material breach of SHA/PIIA, criminal conviction, or voluntary resignation before the 1-year cliff.
     - *Consequence*: Unvested shares forfeited immediately; vested shares repurchased by company at nominal value (e.g., $0.01/share) or book value.
   - **Good Leaver**: Death, permanent disability, involuntary termination without cause, or mutually agreed resignation after the 1-year cliff.
     - *Consequence*: Unvested shares forfeited; vested shares retained or repurchased by company at Fair Market Value (FMV) determined by independent valuation or recent priced round, payable over a 12 to 24-month installment schedule.

### Step 5: Exit & Liquidity Governance (Drag-Along & Tag-Along)
1. **Drag-Along Right**: If shareholders holding $\ge 75\%$ of voting stock (including a majority of founder shares) approve a bona fide third-party acquisition offer, all remaining shareholders must vote in favor and sell their shares on identical terms.
2. **Tag-Along Right**: If any founder proposes to sell shares to a third party, minority shareholders have the right to participate in the sale pro-rata at the identical valuation, price per share, and terms.
3. **Right of First Refusal (ROFR)**: Any proposed secondary share transfer must first be offered to the company and remaining shareholders at the third-party offer price.

### Step 6: Restrictive Covenants (Non-Compete & Exclusivity)
1. **Full-Time Devotion**: Founders must devote 100% of their business time and efforts to the company. External consulting or board seats require unanimous shareholder consent.
2. **Non-Compete**: Founders agree not to establish, consult for, or invest in a directly competing business for a period of 12 to 24 months post-departure.
3. **Non-Solicitation**: 24-month ban on soliciting company employees, contractors, or active customers.
4. **Liquidated Damages**: Insert an enforceable penalty clause for willful breach of restrictive covenants to ensure immediate judicial enforceability.

   - Monthly Executive Decision Meeting Protocol & Accountability Log.
   - Shareholders' Agreement (SHA) Architecture (4-Year Vesting, Double-Trigger).
   - Good Leaver vs. Bad Leaver Trigger Matrix and Repurchase Formulas.
   - Transfer Restrictions (Drag-Along, Tag-Along, ROFR, Non-Compete).
