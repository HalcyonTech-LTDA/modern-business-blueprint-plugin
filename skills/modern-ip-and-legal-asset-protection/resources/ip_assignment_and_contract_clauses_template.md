# IP Assignment & Contract Clauses Template Library

This library contains institutional, attorney-grade model contract clauses for the Corporate Architect Agent to enforce 100% intellectual property ownership and protect the enterprise software company across contractor, agency, and customer relationships.

---

## 1. Absolute Contractor / Outsourced Agency IP Assignment Clause

```text
Section [X]. Intellectual Property Assignment & Chain of Title.

(a) Present Assignment. Contractor hereby unconditionally, irrevocably, and exclusively assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company all worldwide right, title, and interest in and to any and all inventions, works of authorship, software, algorithms, source code, object code, designs, technical architectures, rules engines, data schemas, trade secrets, and related intellectual property rights (collectively, the "Deliverables") conceived, developed, reduced to practice, or authored by Contractor (alone or jointly with others) in connection with the services provided to the Company, whether created prior to, on, or following the effective date of this Agreement.

(b) Work Made for Hire. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, all copyrightable Deliverables created by Contractor shall be deemed "works made for hire."

(c) Waiver of Moral Rights. Contractor hereby unconditionally and irrevocably waives, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, any and all moral rights (including rights of paternity, attribution, and integrity) that Contractor may have in or to any Deliverables.

(d) Custody and Repository Turnover. Contractor covenants that all source code, technical documentation, deployment scripts, and configuration assets shall be committed directly to version control repositories and cloud infrastructure accounts owned and controlled exclusively by the Company. Contractor shall not retain any copies, forks, or operational control over the Deliverables following termination.

(e) Background Technology License. To the extent any pre-existing intellectual property of Contractor is incorporated into any Deliverable, Contractor hereby grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up, transferable, sublicensable license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, display, and commercialize such pre-existing technology as part of the Deliverables.
```

## 2. Enterprise SaaS MSA — Intellectual Property Reservation Clause

```text
Section [Y]. Proprietary Rights & Telemetry.

(a) Reservation of Rights. Except for the limited access rights expressly granted herein, the Company and its licensors retain all right, title, and interest (including all patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, and other intellectual property rights) in and to the Platform, software, APIs, documentation, heuristics, rules engines, algorithms, and any modifications or derivative works thereof. Customer acquires no ownership rights in or to the Platform.

(b) Customer Data. Customer retains all right, title, and interest in and to all electronic data, information, and records submitted by Customer into the Platform ("Customer Data"). Customer grants the Company a limited, non-exclusive license to process Customer Data solely as necessary to provide the Services during the Term.

(c) Aggregated & De-Identified Telemetry. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, the Company may collect, aggregate, and analyze anonymized usage metrics, system performance telemetry, and operational data derived from the operation of the Platform ("Aggregated Data"). The Company may use Aggregated Data for purposes of security monitoring, benchmark reporting, system optimization, and machine learning model training, provided that such Aggregated Data does not identify Customer or any individual data subject.
```

## 3. Standard Mutual Limitation of Liability Clause

```text
Section [Z]. Limitation of Liability.

(a) Consequential Damages Waiver. NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER PARTY FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, PUNITIVE, OR EXEMPLARY DAMAGES, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO LOSS OF REVENUE, PROFITS, GOODWILL, USE, DATA, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION, ARISING OUT OF OR IN CONNECTION WITH THIS AGREEMENT, REGARDLESS OF THE THEORY OF LIABILITY (WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT, NEGLIGENCE, STRICT LIABILITY, OR OTHERWISE), EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

(b) Aggregate Liability Cap. EXCEPT FOR A PARTY’S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION [INDEMNITY], WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE STRICTLY LIMITED TO THE TOTAL FEES ACTUALLY PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER TO THE COMPANY UNDER THE APPLICABLE ORDER FORM IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTH PERIOD IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.
```

## 4. Service Level Agreement — Sole and Exclusive Remedy Clause

```text
Section [SLA]. Service Credits & Exclusivity of Remedy.

In the event the Platform fails to meet the Monthly Uptime Percentage commitment of 99.9%, Customer shall be eligible to receive a Service Credit in accordance with the SLA Schedule, provided Customer submits a formal claim within thirty (30) days of the end of the affected billing month. 

THE ISSUANCE OF SERVICE CREDITS AS SET FORTH HEREIN SHALL BE CUSTOMER'S SOLE AND EXCLUSIVE FINANCIAL REMEDY, AND THE COMPANY'S ENTIRE LIABILITY, FOR ANY FAILURE OF THE PLATFORM TO MEET THE AVAILABILITY OR PERFORMANCE COMMITMENTS. IN NO EVENT SHALL THE TOTAL SERVICE CREDITS ISSUED IN ANY GIVEN CALENDAR MONTH EXCEED TWENTY PERCENT (20%) OF THE MONTHLY SUBSCRIPTION FEE.
```
