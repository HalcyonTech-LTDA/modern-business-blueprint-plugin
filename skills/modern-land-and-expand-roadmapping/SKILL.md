# Land and Expand Roadmapping

This skill provides the architectural frameworks, expansion modeling, and product evolution methodology for the **Product & GTM Lead Agent** (VP of Product & CRO) to design a compounding enterprise land-and-expand product trajectory that drives $\ge 120\% - 130\%$ Net Revenue Retention (NRR).

---

## Core Principles

1. **The Four Vectors of Enterprise Expansion**:
   - Expansion within enterprise accounts does not occur by chance; it is programmatically architected across four vectors:
     - **Vector 1: Throughput / Volume Expansion**: Growth in the customer's core business metrics (transactions, active accounts, monitored entities, API calls) naturally escalates billable tier consumption under Layer 2 and Layer 3 pricing.
     - **Vector 2: Intra-Account Modular Expansion**: Unlocking adjacent operational modules that consume the exact same data substrate already ingested by the beachhead wedge.
     - **Vector 3: Cross-Departmental Adoption**: Transitioning the platform from the initial buying team (e.g., Compliance) into adjacent departments (e.g., Fraud Operations, Credit Underwriting, Legal Risk) through shared entity graphs and audit ledgers.
     - **Vector 4: Cross-Vertical Migration**: Transferring the proven horizontal platform into adjacent industry sectors that share identical workflow topologies (e.g., expanding from Fintech Remittance $\rightarrow$ Digital Banking $\rightarrow$ Crypto Exchanges $\rightarrow$ Healthcare Insurance Clearing).

2. **The Horizontal Core Engine with Configurable Solutions Invariant**:
   - **Rule 3 Enforcement**: Enterprise startups die when they say "yes" to custom client feature requests, turning themselves into low-margin systems integrators.
   - The platform architecture must enforce strict separation:
     - **Immutable Horizontal Core Engine**: High-performance transaction streaming, unified entity graph, distributed rule evaluation, state management, cryptographic audit logging, IAM, and enterprise tenancy.
     - **Configurable Solution Layer**: Declarative YAML/JSON domain schemas, visual policy builders, standardized integration connectors, and workflow routing rules.
   - Every vertical or customer variation must be achievable purely through configuration. If a customer requires custom code, the feature is either abstracted into the core or rejected.

3. **Data Gravity as the Expansion Catalyst**:
   - The initial Trojan Horse wedge captures operational telemetry that no competing point solution possesses.
   - When the enterprise considers solving an adjacent problem (e.g., chargeback disputes), building on top of the already-integrated platform costs 80% less time and money than purchasing and integrating a new vendor.
   - The platform leverages accumulated context to make switching away catastrophic and adding modules effortless.

4. **Programmatic Expansion Triggers**:
   - Rather than relying on cold sales calls for upsells, expansion conversations must be triggered by automated platform telemetry:
     - 80% volume threshold warnings.
     - Cross-departmental data querying anomalies.
     - High-value correlated signals detected across unmonitored workflows.

### Step 1: Map the Trojan Horse Wedge Footprint
Document the baseline integration established during beachhead landing:
- What live event streams are captured?
- What internal ledgers or databases are being read or updated?
- Which operational users interact with the system daily?

### Step 2: Map Adjacent Operational Modules (Phase 2 Expansion)
Identify the natural next steps in the customer's operational pipeline:
- *Example (Fintech)*:
  - *Beachhead Wedge*: Real-Time Sanctions Screening.
  - *Module 2 (Months 6-12)*: Continuous AML Transaction Monitoring.
  - *Module 3 (Months 12-18)*: Automated Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) Filing.
  - *Module 4 (Months 18-24)*: Fraud Ring & Syndicate Graph Intelligence.
- Every subsequent module must reuse $\ge 80\%$ of the data pipeline established in Phase 1.

### Step 3: Enforce Horizontal Engine vs Config Schema Decoupling
Audit the roadmap against **Rule 3 (Constraint)**:
- Verify that Phase 2, 3, and 4 modules leverage the existing horizontal engine.
- Define the configuration schema primitives required to power each module:
  - Policy DSL / Expression syntax.
  - State machine transition definitions.
  - Webhook and callback routing targets.

### Step 4: Program Automated Commercial Expansion Triggers
Establish explicit product telemetry milestones that notify the Customer Success and Account Management teams:
- *Trigger 1 (Capacity)*: Customer reaches 85% of contracted Layer 2 active entities $\rightarrow$ Automatic renewal tier step-up.
- *Trigger 2 (Signal Discovery)*: System flags high-frequency anomaly in unmonitored transaction channel $\rightarrow$ Automated alert demonstrating value of Module 2.
- *Trigger 3 (Multi-User Expansion)*: Second business unit queries the entity graph via API $\rightarrow$ Triggers cross-departmental enterprise license discussion.

### Step 5: Sequence Vertical Migration & Market Compounding
Map the multi-year horizontal expansion across industry verticals based on:
1. *Workflow Parity*: How closely the new vertical's data matches the core engine schema.
2. *Regulatory Convergence*: Whether regulations in the new vertical mirror the beachhead (e.g., FinCEN in banking $\rightarrow$ FinCEN in crypto $\rightarrow$ CMS in healthcare).
3. *Sales Referenceability*: Leveraging early enterprise case studies to establish immediate credibility.
