# Investment Thesis Evaluation Checklist

This checklist is used by Tier-1 Venture Capital Partners and Investment Committees to evaluate the commercial rigor and defensibility of a company conception thesis.

---

## 1. Thesis & Market Dynamics (Mandatory 4/4)

- [ ] **Structural Inflection Point**: Does the thesis identify a non-cosmetic macroeconomic, regulatory, or architectural dislocation rather than generic market growth?
- [ ] **Incumbent Cannibalization Barrier**: Are legacy competitors structurally unable to respond without destroying their existing high-margin revenue model?
- [ ] **Wedge Precision**: Does the Trojan Horse wedge solve a bleeding-neck critical-path operational pain rather than a discretionary workflow luxury?
- [ ] **Category Monopoly Potential**: Does the positioning establish an ownable category boundary with clear anti-positioning ("What it is NOT")?

---

## 2. Financial & Unit Economics Proof (Mandatory 5/5)

- [ ] **P&L Software Segregation**: Are third-party data costs and cloud hosting strictly segregated to prove software gross margins $\ge 80\%$?
- [ ] **CAC Payback Velocity**: Is the fully loaded CAC payback period proven or modeled at $\le 12\text{ months}$ based on gross margin contribution?
- [ ] **Compounding Retention**: Is Net Revenue Retention (NRR) $\ge 120\% - 130\%$ supported by concrete expansion mechanisms (monitored entities or modules)?
- [ ] **Capital-Efficient Runway**: Does the proposed funding tranche provide at least 12 to 18 months of operating runway to extinguish the current gate?
- [ ] **Infrastructure Ratio**: Do direct cloud hosting and third-party data provider fees consume $\le 15\% - 20\%$ of total revenue?

---

## 3. Non-Feature Defensibility (Mandatory 4/4)

- [ ] **Operational Integration Gravity**: Is the software embedded into critical transaction paths where rip-and-replace risks operational shutdown?
- [ ] **Statutory System of Record**: Does the software provide cryptographic audit trails required for regulatory defense and statutory reporting?
- [ ] **Derived Data Network Effects**: Does every incremental transaction or customer improve decision accuracy for all other platform participants?
- [ ] **Accumulated Historical Context**: Does switching vendors cause catastrophic loss of historical calibration weights and compliance context?

---

## 4. Underwriting & Returns Feasibility (Mandatory 3/3)

- [ ] **Institutional MOIC Target**: Does the base case financial underwriting demonstrate at least $5.0\times - 10.0\times$ return on invested capital?
- [ ] **Realistic Exit Multiple**: Are exit valuations anchored in standard institutional software multiples ($8\times - 12\times$ ARR) rather than bubble multiples?
- [ ] **Execution Roadmap Discipline**: Does the plan feature a pragmatic 18-month execution roadmap with binary, milestone-driven decision gates?
