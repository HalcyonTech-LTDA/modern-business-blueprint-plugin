# Multi-Agent Architecture: Modern Business Blueprint System

This document outlines the multi-agent choreography, role allocation, communication protocols, and quality gates governing the **Modern Business Blueprint Plugin**.

---

## 1. System Objectives

The Modern Business Blueprint multi-agent system automates the transition from raw, fragmented ideas into comprehensive, institutional-grade company conception blueprints. It coordinates specialized agents through structured handoffs, ensuring that no technical asset is proposed without economic justification and no business model is approved without stress-tested defensibility.

---

## 3. Sequential Workflow Execution Choreography

```
[Raw Idea Ingestion]
       │
       ▼
[Stage 1: Lead Orchestrator] ──> Normalizes prompt and dispatches Hypothesis Context
       │
       ▼
[Stage 2: Business Strategist Agent]
       ├── Activates "Category Design & Positioning"
       │   └── Formulates "I. A TESE CENTRAL", "II. A EMPRESA", and IS vs IS NOT Boundary
       ├── Activates "Monetization & Economic Architecture"
       │   └── Designs 4-layer revenue model & models unit economics
       └── Activates "Moat & Defensibility Strategy"
           └── Architects switching costs, network effects, derived data, and history graph
       │
       ▼
[Stage 3: Systems & Architecture Agent]
       ├── Audits proposed infrastructure against Rule 3 (Zero tech for tech's sake)
       ├── Ties every pipeline/database to monetizable tier or COGS reduction
       └── Projects gross margin expansion curve (Phase 1 -> Phase 3)
       │
       ▼
[Stage 4: Go-to-Market & Operations Agent]
       ├── Formulates ICP qualification & sales cycle benchmarks
       └── Details programmatic consumption expansion mechanics (NRR > 120%)
       │
       ▼
[Stage 5: Red-Team Investment Partner]
       ├── Conducts Fast-Follower and "Free" Bundling Stress Tests
       └── Issues Investment Committee Pass/Fail Hurdle Scores
       │
       ▼
[Stage 6: Blueprint Orchestrator Agent]
       ├── Enforces Rule 1 (Tone): Purges startup buzzwords and verifies raw candor
       ├── Enforces Rule 2 (Structure): Validates mandatory Markdown header standard
       └── Emits Final Institutional Conception Blueprint
```

## 4. Operational Invariants for Multi-Agent Collaboration

1. **State Immutability**: Each agent receives the immutable state of upstream outputs and emits an additive, structured Markdown section.
2. **Deterministic Veto Power**: The Red-Team Investment Partner and Systems Agent possess absolute veto authority:
   - If the Systems Agent detects technology lacking economic justification (violating Rule 3), the component is excised.
   - If the Red-Team Partner detects low defensibility ($< 15/20$) or negative unit economics, the blueprint is returned to the Business Strategist Agent for redesign.
3. **Executive Voice Consistency**: The Lead Orchestrator guarantees that the voice across all generated sections maintains the strict executive candor and cold analytical perspective of a Tier-1 Venture Capital Partner.
